# Hi, I'm Aykut

IT Operations & Platform Support Specialist based in Istanbul.

For the last 6+ years I've worked in IT operations and support: first on retail systems at Persis, then supporting 500+ DeFacto stores across several countries, and now monitoring Virtual POS and payment traffic at RoofStacks. Most of my day is monitoring, incident handling and working with engineering teams, banks and payment providers until things are back to normal.

Before IT operations I spent close to ten years running my own e-commerce business and doing software consulting and web management. That's where I learned to care about the customer on the other end of every outage.

Lately I've been building small tools and a side product with AI-assisted development (Claude Code).

### What I work with

- Incident, problem and change management (ITIL), SLA and FCR tracking, escalations
- Monitoring and log analysis with Grafana and Kibana / Elastic Stack
- POS, checkout and Virtual POS systems, multi-country store rollouts
- Microsoft 365, Active Directory, Jira Service Management
- Next.js and TypeScript for my own projects

### Projects

**[Crewlyne](https://github.com/aoyekcin/crewlyne)**: a field service and maintenance app for HVAC and facility maintenance teams. The office plans work orders and sees device history; technicians record the job on their phone and send a PDF service report before leaving the site. Live at [crewlyne.com](https://crewlyne.com).

**[it-ops-runbooks](https://github.com/aoyekcin/it-ops-runbooks)**: the runbooks and templates I'd want on day one in any IT ops team. Severity and escalation matrices, store POS and network outage runbooks, payment error triage, postmortem and change templates.

**[it-ops-powershell](https://github.com/aoyekcin/it-ops-powershell)**: PowerShell scripts for routine admin work: AD onboarding/offboarding, stale computer cleanup, M365 license review, Intune compliance and multi-site connectivity checks. Anything that changes something supports `-WhatIf`.
